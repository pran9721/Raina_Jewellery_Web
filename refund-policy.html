Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellation, return and refund requests are handled.

Cancellation requests

Contact customer support as soon as possible with your order number. Requests may not be possible once an order has been dispatched.

Returns

Return eligibility depends on the product condition and the store’s confirmed return terms. Keep the product, packaging and invoice until your request is resolved.

Refunds

Approved refunds for online payments are processed through the original payment method or payment provider process. Bank processing times may vary.

Damaged or incorrect items

Contact support promptly with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging.
