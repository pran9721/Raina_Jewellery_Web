Privacy Policy

This page explains how RAINA JEWELS uses the information needed to operate the store.

Information we collect

When you create an account, place an order or contact us, the store may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order details.

How information is used

Information is used to manage accounts, process orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support, prevent misuse and maintain store records.

Payments

Online payments are handled by the selected payment gateway. RAINA JEWELS should not store full card numbers, CVV values or payment passwords.

Data protection

We use reasonable safeguards to protect account and order information. Do not share your password or one-time passwords with anyone.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact Us page.
